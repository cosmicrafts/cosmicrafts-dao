## ART STYLES

### SUGGESTED AD01 - "STYLIZED"
- REDUCED IN NUMBER AND BIGGER, STYLIZED DETAILS
- CLEAN AND READABLE TEXTURES AND MATERIALS
- ENLARGED PROPORTIONS
- GOOD READABILITY
- CLEAR COLOR CODING

*Example: Sleek, angular spaceships with glowing energy lines, reduced panel details but clear silhouettes*

### SUGGESTED AD02 - "CARTOON"
- LESS IN NUMBER, BIG AND EXAGGERATED DETAILS
- CLEAN AND READABLE TEXTURES AND MATERIALS
- HIGHLY EXAGGERATED PROPORTIONS
- HIGH READABILITY
- CLEAR COLOR CODING

*Example: Bulbous, oversized engine thrusters, exaggerated weapon systems, bubble-like cockpits*

### SUGGESTED AD03 - "STYLIZED/CARTOON HYBRID"
- LESS IN NUMBER, BIG AND EXAGGERATED DETAILS
- CLEAN AND READABLE TEXTURES AND MATERIALS
- HIGHLY EXAGGERATED PROPORTIONS
- HIGH READABILITY
- CLEAR COLOR CODING

*Example: Maintains stylized sleekness but with exaggerated key elements like weapons and engines*

### CURRENT AD - "REALISTIC"
- MANY IN NUMBER AND SMALL IN SIZE REALISTIC DETAILS
- LOT'S OF NOISE IN TEXTURES AND MATERIALS
- REALISTIC PROPORTIONS
- REDUCED READABILITY FROM BIG DISTANCE
- HARD TO MAKE CONVINCING COLOR CODING (WITHOUT LOOKING PLASTIC AND TOYISH)

*Example: Highly detailed spacecraft with realistic panel lines, greebling, wear and tear textures*

## ENVIRONMENT

**GRID SPACES:** Clean, hexagonal or square grid spaces with subtle holographic outlines

**SPACE BACKGROUND:** Deep space with subtle nebula effects, not overpowering gameplay elements

**CELESTIAL OBJECTS:** Stylized planets, asteroids, and space stations serving as terrain

**EFFECTS:** Bright, readable laser beams, shield effects, and explosions with clear gameplay impact

---

## UNITS/SPACECRAFT

**SCALE HIERARCHY:**
- Small units: Scout ships, fighters, drones (small profile)
- Medium units: Frigates, destroyers, specialized vessels
- Large units: Capital ships, carriers, motherships

**FACTION COLOR CODING:**
- Faction 1: Blue/Cyan energy signatures
- Faction 2: Red/Orange energy signatures 
- Faction 3: Green/Yellow energy signatures
- Neutral: Purple/White energy signatures

**IMPORTANT NOTE:** Silhouettes must be instantly recognizable at a glance for quick strategic decisions

---

## CHARACTERS/PILOTS

**PILOT DESIGNS:**
- Simplified, color-coded according to faction
- Recognizable stereotypes (engineer, commander, tactical officer)
- Exaggerated proportions for readability in UI elements

**ANIMATION:**
- Limited but expressive animations for UI portraits
- Focus on key emotional states (normal, happy, concerned, angry)

---

## STRUCTURES

**BUILDING DESIGN:**
- Clear shape language differentiating building types
- Construction/upgrade states visually obvious
- Faction-specific architectural styles while maintaining readability

**RESOURCE STRUCTURES:**
- Energy collectors: Bright, glowing elements
- Mining facilities: Clear extraction animations
- Research facilities: Distinctive antenna/satellite features

---

## UI ELEMENTS

**HUD:**
- Minimal, clean interface
- Important information highlighted with glow effects
- Critical alerts use animation to draw attention

**TECH TREES:**
- Clear iconography for technologies
- Visual progression showing upgrade paths
- Locked/available states instantly readable

---

## COLOR PALETTE

**RECOMMENDED BASE COLORS:**
- Space background: Deep blues and purples (RGB: 8,12,30)
- UI elements: Clean whites and light blues (RGB: 220,230,255)
- Selection indicators: Bright cyan (RGB: 0,255,255)
- Warning indicators: Bright orange (RGB: 255,140,0)

**FACTION-SPECIFIC PALETTES:**
- Use consistent color schemes across all faction assets
- Maintain color brightness hierarchy for gameplay clarity
- Ensure all colors remain readable against space backgrounds

---

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

**POLYCOUNT TARGETS:**
- Small units: 1500-3000 tris
- Medium units: 3000-6000 tris
- Large units: 6000-12000 tris
- Environment pieces: 500-2000 tris

**TEXTURE SIZES:**
- Small units: 512x512
- Medium units: 1024x1024
- Large units: 2048x2048
- UI elements: Appropriate for target resolutions, vector when possible

**ANIMATION GUIDELINES:**
- Smooth, clear movement patterns
- Weapon effects highly visible
- Death/destruction animations satisfying but not obstructing gameplay

---

## VISUAL REFERENCES AND INSPIRATION

**SUCCESSFUL EXAMPLES:**
- Homeworld series (for elegant spacecraft design)
- StarCraft II (for readable unit silhouettes)
- Clash Royale (for mobile-friendly art style)
- EVE Echoes (for mobile space aesthetic)

**AVOID:**
- Overly complex detailing that gets lost at game camera distance
- Realistic textures that reduce readability
- Similar silhouettes between different unit types
- Low contrast between units and backgrounds
